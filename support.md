## Frequently Asked Questions

### What can I do with Chancilio?

Chancilio helps you make random choices with a spinning wheel, random numbers, coin tosses, dice, prize draws, random groups, and a finger picker.

### How many free uses do I get?

Free users receive five uses per day, shared across all tools. The daily allowance resets at local midnight.

### How do I unlock unlimited use?

Chancilio offers a one-time purchase for unlimited use. It is not a subscription and has no recurring fee.

### How do I restore my purchase?

Open the purchase screen and tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you are signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

A successful purchase or restoration closes the purchase screen and updates your membership status.

### My purchase is not recognized. What should I do?

Check your internet connection and Apple Account, then restart Chancilio. The app checks your purchase entitlement when it starts.

If access is still unavailable, try **Restore Purchases** or contact us.

### Can I use my own images for the coin?

Yes. Open **Coin toss** and tap the customization button. Choose an image for each side, then drag and zoom to crop it into a circle.

Both sides have an equal 50% chance, regardless of the images you choose.

### Where is my app data stored?

Your saved content and custom coin images are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app may remove this data.

### How do I request a refund?

Apple handles payments and refund requests. Visit [Apple’s Report a Problem website](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/) to request assistance.
